# Theme artwork masters

These ten pairs of illustration masters were created for Gloam Shell NDX with
agent-assisted image generation. The project offers its rights in this artwork
under CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The PNGs are production authoring assets. Each `*-bottom.png` is a separate
companion composition, with the same setting, palette and materials as its top
illustration. `tools/make_theme_scenes.py` prepares both at native 256×192 indexed
resolution. Reading surfaces remain separate to protect text contrast. Prism's broad color shapes are original vector-style artwork
implemented in that tool under the same CC0 dedication.

No image, character, logo or skin from another launcher's theme is included.
Generation prompts and working drafts remain outside the public source tree.

Each of the eleven `*-icons.png` files is a separate original 512×512 RGBA
atlas, with sixteen objects in a 4×4 grid. `tools/make_theme_icons.py` fits those
objects into native 32×32 sprites and maps related entry points within each
pack. They share the CC0 dedication above. The motifs, silhouettes and materials
were authored independently for each theme; native title banners remain separate.

`materials.png` is an original agent-assisted 4×3 material atlas, offered under
the same CC0 dedication. It contains painted metal, felt, rag paper, indigo
cotton, sailcloth, canvas, satin, ceramic, leather, expedition paper, velvet and
linen studies. It contains no texture copied from iOS or another product.
`tools/theme_materials.py` prepares complete native-size image crops from these studies,
with separate ordinary/focus material fields. It does not repeat small patches.
