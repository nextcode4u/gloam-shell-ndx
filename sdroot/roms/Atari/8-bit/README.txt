Atari/8-bit
========================================

SD folder: /roms/Atari/8-bit/

Content: .atr, .xex

Application: A8DS

Argv in the audited upstream build: direct.

Firmware: Built-in Altirra OS 3.41 and BASIC 1.58. Optional originals:
atarixl.rom, atariosb.rom, ataribas.rom, a5200.rom in /roms/bios or
/data/bios.

Add-on package: available; terms: GPL-2.0-or-later + Altirra notices.

Upstream: https://github.com/wavemotion-dave/A8DS
Release: https://github.com/wavemotion-dave/A8DS/releases/tag/4.2

The default profile maps .atr and .xex. Other formats supported by A8DS, such
as .atx and .car, may be selected in its own browser; no generic .rom
association is added.

Keep required companion files and saves together. Other emulator-supported
formats may need its own browser or a per-file application choice. Changing an
extension is not a file-format conversion.

Full setup and compatibility audit:
https://github.com/nextcode4u/gloam-shell-ndx/blob/main/docs/guides/ADDONS.md
