ZX Spectrum
========================================

SD folder: /roms/ZX Spectrum/

Content: .z80, .tap, .tzx

Application: SpeccySE

Argv in the audited upstream build: direct.

Firmware: Requires 48.ROM and 128.ROM beside emulator or in /roms/bios. OpenSE
BASIC is a candidate for 48K only, not a verified full replacement for this
required pair.

Add-on package: available; terms: Custom noncommercial redistribution + core
notices.

Upstream: https://github.com/wavemotion-dave/SpeccySE
Release: https://github.com/wavemotion-dave/SpeccySE/releases/tag/2.2

Keep required companion files and saves together. Other emulator-supported
formats may need its own browser or a per-file application choice. Changing an
extension is not a file-format conversion.

Full setup and compatibility audit:
https://github.com/nextcode4u/gloam-shell-ndx/blob/main/docs/guides/ADDONS.md
